# Getnet Multi-Agent Support Context

Domain language for supporting Getnet merchants with payment products, point-of-sale hardware troubleshooting, financial settlements, and general utility inquiries.

## Core Entities & Actors

**Merchant**:
A business or individual entrepreneur registered with Getnet (identified by `user_id`, e.g., `cliente1988`) who accepts payments and receives settlements.
_Avoid_: Client, user, customer, account holder

**POS Terminal**:
A physical card machine (such as *Get Clássica* or *Get Smart*) leased or purchased by a Merchant to process in-person card and Pix transactions.
_Avoid_: Card reader, device, hardware, maquininha (except when quoting Brazilian product names)

**Inquiry**:
An incoming message submitted by a Merchant seeking product knowledge, account-specific support, or general utility information.
_Avoid_: Prompt, ticket, request, chat

## Financial & Payment Products

**Settlement**:
The scheduled payout of net funds from a Merchant's completed sales into their registered bank account or Getnet digital account.
_Avoid_: Deposit, payout, transfer, payment

**Receivables Advance**:
A financial service (*Antecipação de Recebíveis*) allowing a Merchant to receive funds from credit or installment sales before the standard Settlement date, subject to a fee.
_Avoid_: Loan, early payout, cash advance

**Crediário**:
A Getnet payment modality enabling a Merchant's buyer to split a purchase into extended installments (up to 24–36x depending on issuer rules) financed by the card issuer while the Merchant receives funds on a predictable schedule.
_Avoid_: Standard installments, parcelado lojista, BNPL

**Payment Link**:
A digital checkout URL (*Link de Pagamento* / *Get Pay*) generated by a Merchant to collect card, Pix, or boleto payments remotely via messaging channels such as WhatsApp or social media without a website.
_Avoid_: Checkout page, invoice link, payment button

## Support & Governance Concepts

**Safety Policy**:
The three-tier classification governing which Inquiries are processed:
1. *Getnet Domain & Support* (product rules, POS troubleshooting, Merchant settlements),
2. *General Utility* (benign real-time factual queries such as weather or exchange rates), and
3. *Restricted / Unsafe* (prompt injections, jailbreaks, cross-merchant data access, or harmful content).
_Avoid_: Filter, moderation, firewall

**Transaction Decline**:
A rejected payment attempt on a POS Terminal or Payment Link, categorized by an issuer, network, or terminal connectivity error code.
_Avoid_: Failed payment, error, chargeback

**Support Ticket**:
A recorded operational issue linked to a Merchant and optionally a POS Terminal when automated self-service cannot immediately resolve a technical or financial problem.
_Avoid_: Case, bug report, log

**Human Handoff**:
The structured transfer of an unresolved, high-severity, or low-confidence Inquiry—along with Merchant context and diagnostic history—to a live human support specialist.
_Avoid_: Fallback, redirect, transfer
