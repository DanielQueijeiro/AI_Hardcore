## What the site publishes about receivables advance
The Getnet help page "Antecipação de vendas" contains only a video section with tips; it does not publish in text the definition, modalities, deadlines, fees, eligible sales or cut-off times for receivables advance (antecipação). What other pages state: receivables advance is one of the functions of the Get Conta digital account; the FAQ fee tables show plans with settlement in 2 business days or "receive as you sell" (Receba conforme suas vendas); and without an advance, merchant installments (Parcelado Lojista) pay the first installment at D+31 and each following one 30 days later. Conditions and rates for a specific merchant must be simulated in the Getnet app or Portal Minha Conta, or confirmed with Getnet support (4002-4000 / 4003-4000 in capitals and metropolitan regions, 0800-648-8000 elsewhere, 06h00 to 22h00).
