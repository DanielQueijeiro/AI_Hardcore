## What Get Conta is
Get Conta is Getnet's digital account, promoted as "digital account and free card" (conta digital e cartão grátis) together with the card machines. It lets a merchant receive all payments in the same account, control the operation from the account, use a free virtual card, and access receivables advance (antecipação de recebíveis). It is managed in the Getnet Brasil app (Google Play and App Store). The page publishes no maintenance fee.

## Opening Get Conta and requirements
Registration uses a CPF (individual) or CNPJ (company), and it is not necessary to already have a Getnet product. The account is opened by choosing a machine or another product (maquininhas, link de pagamento or Get Tap) on the site, by WhatsApp (11 97694-6815) or by phone. The page does not state whether an account at another bank is also required; it only says payments can be received in Get Conta itself.
