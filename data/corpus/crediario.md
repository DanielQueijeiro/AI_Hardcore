## Crediário installment limit
With Crediário a sale can be split into up to 48 installments (parcelamento em até 48x). The maximum term and the interest rate of the installment plan vary according to the card issuer (emissor do cartão). The simulation on the card machine shows the interest, and the issuer approves the transaction.

## Crediário settlement for the merchant
The merchant receives Crediário sales in up to 2 business days (2 dias úteis) after the transaction is captured, in a single payment (de uma única vez) regardless of the number of installments, net of the discount rate. Crediário sales do not count toward the card machine rental waiver.
