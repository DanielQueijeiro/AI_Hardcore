## Machine connection: chip, signal and communication test
The FAQ has no general guide for a machine that will not connect; it documents machines with the Algar chip. The Algar chip is multi-carrier, works on 2G/3G coverage across Brazil and avoids chip swaps. To install it, with the machine off: open the back cover, insert the chip in the slot marked SIM1 or SIM2 with the metal side down, close the cover, turn the machine on and wait for the data connection; when the network (2G or 3G) appears the machine is ready. Communication test (teste de comunicação): press the function key (F, # or another depending on the model), press 60, choose option 2 (manutenção) and then option 1 (teste de comunicação). Power on/off depends on the model: green key, up-arrow key, plug, or holding the green/red key for 5 seconds. If a TEF system is not working, technical support is 4003-5025 at any day and time.

## Declined, duplicated and cancelled sales; chargebacks
The FAQ does not list causes for a declined (recusada) transaction. It documents: on the Get Smart, two identical sales (same card, brand and amount) within 5 minutes cause the second to be cancelled automatically, so wait 5 minutes or use a different amount; cancelling sales from previous days on the Get Smart is done only by phone, 4003-4000. A chargeback (contestação de venda) is when the bank cancels a transaction and debits the merchant, for example for goods not received or defective, a transaction not recognized by the cardholder, duplicate processing, a refund not processed, or payment by another means. Card-not-present sales can be cancelled even if the merchant proves the transaction.

## When sales are paid out (prazos de recebimento)
Published terms: with the 2-business-day plan the debit rate is 1.89% and credit in one payment 3.99%; with the "Receba conforme suas vendas" plan debit is 1.95% and credit in one payment 3.09%. With Get Conta, payment arrives in up to 2 business days, in the morning, when that option is chosen. Prepaid cards: debit in up to 1 business day and credit in up to 2 business days. Issuer installments (Parcelado Emissor): received at once, between 2 and 31 days depending on the plan, up to 24 installments. Merchant installments (Parcelado Lojista): first installment at D+31 and the others 30 days after the previous one, up to 12 installments. Crediário: up to 2 business days after capture, in a single payment. Online store (Loja Digital): credit in one payment 2.80% + R$ 0.90 per transaction, received in 31 days.

## Getnet support channels
Phone for capitals and metropolitan regions: 4002-4000 or 4003-4000. Other locations: 0800-648-8000. Customer service hours: 06h to 22h. WhatsApp for customers: 11 97694-6815. Ombudsman (Ouvidoria): 0800 646 3404, Monday to Friday 9h15 to 17h30 except holidays. Self-service: Portal Minha Conta and the Getnet Brasil app.

## Other FAQ facts
Get Smart battery: charge for at least 3 hours in a row and when it reaches 20%; do not let it fully discharge. Paying with QR Code on the Get Smart: select the debit option. Pre-authorization: up to 30 days to confirm, and the amount can be changed up to 4 times.
