## Get Clássica overview and connectivity
The Get Clássica is described on the Getnet site as a "Maquininha POS 3G + Wi-Fi": a POS card machine that connects over 3G mobile data and Wi-Fi. The page notes that the physical model may vary while the technology stays the same. The page's technical sheet is an image, so keyboard, screen, printer and battery details are not available as text on the page.

## Get Clássica fees (taxas)
Published "from" rates (a partir de) with a single rate (taxa única) for Mastercard, Visa, Elo and Amex: debit (débito) 1.69%; credit in one payment (crédito à vista, 1x) 3.79%; installment credit (crédito parcelado) 2x 7.98%, 3x 8.47%, 4x 8.96%, 5x 9.45%, 6x 9.94%, 7x 10.43%, 8x 10.92%, 9x 11.41%, 10x 11.90%, 11x 12.39%, 12x 12.88%. Card installments on the machine go from 1x to 12x. The page does not publish a Pix rate for this model.

## Get Clássica rental (aluguel) and free-machine conditions
Monthly rental if the minimum sales volume is not reached: Get Clássica R$ 79.90 per month (for comparison: Get Mini R$ 14.90, Get Smart R$ 129.90, selling by phone R$ 0.00). Rental is waived for the Get Clássica with sales of R$ 10 thousand per month. Curation note: the All Getnet card machines page (https://site.getnet.com.br/todas-as-maquininhas/) states R$ 20 thousand per month for the same waiver, so the two Getnet pages disagree and the applicable threshold should be confirmed in the simulator or with Getnet. Voucher (VA/VR) and Crediário sales do not count toward the rental waiver. No purchase price is published.

## Get Clássica settlement (recebimento) and eligibility
Sales are received in 2 business days (2 dias úteis) with Get Conta, the free Getnet digital account and card. For prepaid cards: credit in up to 2 business days and debit in up to 1 business day. The offer applies to individuals (pessoa física), MEI and companies with revenue of up to R$ 3 million per year, for new customers, subject to registration analysis. Existing Getnet customers should simulate rates in the Getnet app.
