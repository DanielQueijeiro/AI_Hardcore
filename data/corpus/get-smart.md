## Get Smart overview: 5-in-1 smart POS
The Get Smart is presented as a "5 in 1" machine: payment terminal, barcode reader (leitor de código de barras), camera, fiscal-receipt printer (impressora) and an integrated management system (sistema de gestão). The site labels it "Maquininha POS 3G + Wi-Fi" (3G and Wi-Fi connectivity). Operating system, screen size and battery specifications are only in an image on the page, not in text.

## Get Smart apps and business features (GetStore)
Get Smart gives access to GetStore, with more than 150 solutions such as cashback, giftback, stock control (controle de estoque) and management systems. Other features: Kiosk mode (Modo Quiosque: one app at a time, screen always on, password protected, suitable for self-service totems); POS TEF Digital (works as a TEF solution integrated with checkout automation); physical split and marketplace for companies, with commission and tip modules where the split happens at transaction time; up to 5 devices can be used in one store (Multi-Equipamentos).

## Get Smart fees (taxas)
Rates "from" (a partir de), single rate for Mastercard, Visa, Elo and Amex, valid for sales received in Get Conta: debit 1.69%; installment credit 2x 7.98%, 3x 8.47%, 4x 8.96%, 5x 9.45%, 6x 9.94%, 7x 10.43%, 8x 10.92%, 9x 11.41%, 10x 11.90%, 11x 12.39%, 12x 12.88%. For credit in one payment (1x) the page shows 3.69% in one section and 3.79% in another and does not clarify which applies. No Pix rate is published for this model.

## Get Smart rental (aluguel) and free-machine conditions
Monthly rental if the minimum sales volume is not reached: R$ 129.90. By monthly sales: from R$ 10 thousand the rental is R$ 50.00; from R$ 30 thousand it is R$ 10.00; from R$ 60 thousand the Get Smart is free. Voucher (VA/VR) and Crediário sales do not count toward the waiver. No purchase price is published.

## Get Smart settlement (recebimento) and eligibility
With Get Conta, sales are received in 2 business days. Prepaid cards: credit in up to 2 business days, debit in up to 1 business day. Terms apply to individuals, MEI and companies with revenue up to R$ 3 million per year, new customers only; the offer cannot be combined with other Getnet or Banco Santander negotiations.

## Get Clássica vs Get Smart: differences
Both are POS machines with 3G + Wi-Fi sold with the same published card rates (debit 1.69%, installments 2x to 12x from 7.98% to 12.88%). The differences the site documents: the Get Smart is a 5-in-1 smart device (payment terminal, barcode reader, camera, printer and management system) with GetStore apps (150+ solutions), Kiosk mode, POS TEF Digital and split payments, while the Get Clássica is the classic POS machine without those app features. Rental without minimum volume is R$ 79.90 per month for the Get Clássica versus R$ 129.90 for the Get Smart, and the rental waiver starts at R$ 10 thousand of monthly sales for the Get Clássica versus R$ 60 thousand for the Get Smart.
