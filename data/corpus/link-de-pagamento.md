## Selling through WhatsApp and social networks
Yes: the Payment Link is made for selling through WhatsApp or social networks ("Faça vendas usando o WhatsApp ou sua rede social"), without a card machine (sem maquininha). The merchant creates a link and shares or copies it to the customer. It includes Getnet's anti-fraud (antifraude) and supports sales to foreigners with an RNE identification document. The page does not say whether the merchant needs a website.

## How to create a Payment Link
Create it in Portal Minha Conta (login and password) or in the Getnet Brasil app: Produtos e Serviços > Link de Pagamento > Criar Link de Pagamento. Fill in the product data, shipping (frete) and payment options, then share or copy the link. Several products can go in the same cart, the link can be personalized with the product image, and shipping can be included in the charge.

## Payment Link payment methods, plans and fees
The buyer can pay by debit, credit, Pix, wallet or boleto. The page does not state a maximum number of card installments for the link. Plans: Inicial is free and charges R$ 1.00 per paid link, only when the payment is approved; Básico costs R$ 28 per month, includes 60 card transactions per month and charges R$ 0.50 per extra transaction. The simulator shows a single rate of 1.90% for Mastercard and Visa. The page gives no standard settlement deadline for the link; the FAQ says D+2 settlement for the payment link becomes available after 6 months of use, subject to analysis.
