## Ways to accept Pix
Getnet accepts Pix in four ways: by approximation (aproximação), by biometrics (biometria, marked as new), with Get Tap on the phone, and by QR Code on the card machine. Pix is available across physical and digital solutions: card machines (maquininhas), payment link (link de pagamento), Get Tap, e-commerce and TEF.

## Pix settlement time (recebimento)
There is no single fixed deadline: the time to receive a Pix sale can vary by up to one calendar day (até um dia corrido), depending on the Pix model the customer is eligible for and the time the sale is approved. The site markets Pix as receiving sales "in seconds".

## Pix requirements, bank account and fees
The Pix page does not say whether a bank account or a Getnet digital account is required to receive Pix sales, does not describe an activation process, and does not publish Pix fees. Its only advice is to keep registration data with Getnet up to date. See the Get Conta digital account page for where sales can be received. Per the FAQ, Pix allows up to 5 keys (chaves) per account for individuals and 20 for companies.
