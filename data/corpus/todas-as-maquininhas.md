## Getnet machine line-up
The line-up page lists three hardware options, Get Mini with paper roll (Get Mini com bobina), Get Clássica (also offered as Get Clássica Santander Select) and Get Smart, plus selling by phone (Venda pelo celular) at R$ 0.00 per month with no physical machine. The page has no technical sheet per model.

## Rental by model on the line-up page
Rental without minimum sales: Get Clássica R$ 79.90 per month, Get Smart R$ 129.90 per month, selling by phone free. This page states rental-waiver thresholds of R$ 20 thousand per month for the Get Clássica and R$ 40 thousand or R$ 80 thousand per month for the Get Smart; these differ from the thresholds on the individual product pages (R$ 10 thousand and R$ 60 thousand), so the applicable threshold should be confirmed in the simulator or with Getnet.
