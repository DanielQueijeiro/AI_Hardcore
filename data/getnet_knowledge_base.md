## 1. POS Terminal Models: Get Clássica vs. Get Smart {#get-classica-vs-get-smart}

Getnet offers different **POS Terminal** models designed for distinct **Merchant** volume and operational profiles. The two flagship countertop and portable receipt-printing models are the **Get Clássica** and the **Get Smart**:

### Get Clássica (Traditional Countertop / Portable POS Terminal)
- **Operating System & Interface**: Dedicated proprietary POS OS (Linux-based) with a color display and a **physical tactile keypad** (ergonomic physical buttons for rapid PIN entry).
- **Best For**: High-throughput retail counters, bakeries, pharmacies, and restaurants that want fast, straightforward card and Pix entry without smart-app complexity.
- **Connectivity**: **3G / 4G mobile data** (free multi-carrier SIM card included) + **Wi-Fi**, plus contactless **NFC** (approximate payment).
- **Receipt Printer**: Built-in high-speed thermal paper coil printer (prints physical customer and merchant receipts).
- **Battery**: Long-lasting rechargeable battery (up to 10–12 hours of continuous use).
- **Accepted Payment Methods**: Credit, Debit, **Crediário**, Voucher/Meal cards (Alelo, Sodexo/Pluxee, Ticket, VR), **Pix** QR Code on screen, and NFC digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay).

### Get Smart (Android Touchscreen Smart POS Terminal)
- **Operating System & Interface**: **Android OS** with a large **HD multi-touch screen** (full touchscreen interface, no physical numeric keypad) and an intuitive smartphone-like experience.
- **Best For**: **Merchants** that want an all-in-one point-of-sale terminal combining payment acceptance with catalog management, inventory control, order splitting, and business apps via the **GetStore** app marketplace.
- **Connectivity**: **Dual-SIM 4G** (automatic carrier switching) + **Dual-band Wi-Fi** + **Bluetooth** + **NFC** contactless reader.
- **Hardware Extras**: Built-in **high-speed thermal receipt printer**, **integrated camera with barcode/QR scanner**, and high-capacity battery designed for full-shift mobility (table-side restaurant billing or delivery).
- **Key Summary of Differences**:
  - **Get Clássica** is a dedicated traditional **POS Terminal** with a **physical keypad** focused strictly on fast, reliable payment processing and receipt printing.
  - **Get Smart** is an **Android touchscreen Smart POS** that adds a camera, **GetStore** business management apps, catalog/inventory software, and Dual-SIM 4G connectivity alongside thermal receipt printing.

## 2. Pix Sales & Bank Account Requirements {#pix-bank-account-rules}

**Do I need a traditional bank account to receive my sales via Pix with Getnet?**
- **No, you do not need a traditional external bank account** to receive your sales via **Pix** (or card sales) with Getnet.
- **Two Settlement Options for Merchants**:
  1. **Getnet Digital Account (*Get Conta* / *SuperGet*)**: If a **Merchant** does not have (or prefers not to use) an external checking or savings account, Getnet provides a free built-in **Getnet digital account**. **Pix** sales and card **Settlement** payouts are credited directly into this digital account, where the **Merchant** can pay bills, make Pix transfers, or withdraw funds using a prepaid Getnet card.
  2. **Registered External Bank Account (Domicílio Bancário)**: **Merchants** who already hold a checking or savings account at **Banco Santander** or any other Brazilian bank / financial institution can register that account as their **Settlement** destination (*domicílio bancário*).
- **Pix Settlement Timeframe**: **Pix** QR Code transactions processed on a Getnet **POS Terminal** (*Pix na Maquininha*) or via **Payment Link** settle in **D+0 (instant / same-day Settlement, 24/7)** directly into the **Merchant**'s chosen Getnet digital account or registered external bank account (such as Banco Santander).
- **Ownership Rule**: Whether using the Getnet digital account or an external bank account, the account must match the **Merchant**'s registered **CPF** (individual entrepreneur) or **CNPJ** (business entity).

## 3. Receivables Advance (Antecipação de Recebíveis) {#receivables-advance-antecipacao}

**How does Receivables Advance (*Antecipação de Recebíveis*) work with Getnet?**
- **Definition**: **Receivables Advance** (*Antecipação de Recebíveis*) is a financial service that allows a **Merchant** to receive the net funds from **credit card sales (single-payment 1x or installments 2x–12x)** before the standard **D+30** **Settlement** cycle (where installments normally pay out every 30 days: 30, 60, 90 days, etc.).
- **Settlement Timeframes with Receivables Advance**:
  - **D+0 (Same Day)**: Receive eligible credit receivables on the very same day the sale occurs or is requested (when contracted before the daily cutoff).
  - **D+1 (Next Business Day)** or **D+2 (Within 2 Business Days)**: Receive the full net value of credit and installment sales in 1 or 2 business days instead of waiting 30 to 360 days.
- **Two Modes of Operation**:
  1. **Automatic Plan (*Antecipação Automática / Plano Flex*)**: Every credit sale made on the **POS Terminal** or **Payment Link** is automatically scheduled for early **Settlement** according to the **Merchant**'s contracted timeframe (e.g., **D+1** or **D+2**).
  2. **On-Demand / Single Advance (*Antecipação Avulsa*)**: The **Merchant** selects specific future receivables batches to advance whenever extra working capital is needed.
- **Fee Structure & Calculation**:
  - A transparent discount rate (*taxa de antecipação*, proportional to the number of days advanced) is deducted from the gross credit sale amount together with the standard MDR (*taxa de intermediação*).
  - Unlike a traditional bank loan, **Receivables Advance** uses the **Merchant**'s own completed credit sales, so it does not consume credit lines or charge IOF tax.
- **How to Contract or Manage**: **Merchants** can simulate rates and activate or pause **Receivables Advance** anytime through the **App Getnet**, **Portal Minha Conta Getnet**, WhatsApp official channel, or Central de Relacionamento.

## 4. Crediário Installment Limits & Mechanics {#crediario-installment-limits}

**How many installments can I split a sale into with the Getnet Crediário?**
- **Installment Limits**: With Getnet **Crediário** (*Crediário na Maquininha*), a **Merchant** can split a sale into **up to 24x or up to 36x installments** (commonly **up to 24 installments**, and **up to 36 installments** depending on the cardholder's issuing bank rules, such as Banco Santander and participating issuers). Standard credit card installment plans (*parcelado lojista/emissor*) normally cap at 12x, whereas **Crediário** extends payment plans up to **24x–36x**.
- **How Crediário Works**:
  1. At checkout on the **POS Terminal** (*Get Clássica* or *Get Smart*), the **Merchant** enters the sale amount, selects the **Crediário** modality, and chooses the number of installments (from **2x up to 24x–36x**).
  2. The **POS Terminal** displays an instant simulation on screen showing the exact installment value and issuer interest conditions for the buyer before the PIN is entered.
  3. **Issuer Financing & Merchant Settlement**: The extended installment plan is financed by the buyer's card issuer. Meanwhile, the **Merchant** receives the **Settlement** of the sale on a short, predictable schedule (typically within **D+2** or up to **30 days** in a single payout, depending on the contracted plan) without waiting 24 or 36 months.

## 5. Selling via WhatsApp with the Getnet Payment Link (Get Pay) {#payment-link-whatsapp}

**Can I sell through WhatsApp using the Getnet Payment Link?**
- **Yes!** **Merchants** can sell remotely through **WhatsApp**, Instagram, Facebook, SMS, or email using the Getnet **Payment Link** (*Link de Pagamento* / **Get Pay**), with **no e-commerce website required**.
- **How to Generate and Share a Payment Link over WhatsApp**:
  1. Open the **App Getnet** or **Portal Minha Conta Getnet** and select **Link de Pagamento (Get Pay)**.
  2. Enter the product/service description, total sale amount, and configure the allowed payment methods and maximum installments.
  3. Generate the secure checkout URL and tap **Share via WhatsApp** (or copy and paste the link directly into the customer's WhatsApp chat).
  4. The buyer clicks the **Payment Link** in WhatsApp, opens a secure Getnet-hosted checkout page in their mobile browser, and completes the payment.
- **Supported Payment Methods on Payment Link**:
  - **Credit Card** (1x or installments **up to 12x**),
  - **Debit Card** and **Wallet payments**,
  - **Pix** (instant QR Code / Pix Copia e Cola with **D+0** **Settlement**),
  - **Boleto Bancário**.
- **Built-in Security**: Every **Payment Link** includes automated **anti-fraud screening** and **3DS 2.0 authentication** to protect the **Merchant** against unauthorized card-not-present chargebacks.

## 6. POS Terminal Network & Connectivity Troubleshooting {#pos-terminal-connectivity-troubleshooting}

**What should I do when my Getnet POS Terminal (card machine) won't connect to the internet?**
When a **POS Terminal** (such as **Get Smart** or **Get Clássica**) fails to connect to the internet or shows `OFFLINE_GPRS_TIMEOUT` / connection error, follow these official Getnet step-by-step reconnection procedures:

1. **Quick Soft Reboot & Signal Refresh**:
   - Hold the **Power** button for 5 seconds and select **Restart (Reiniciar)** (or turn off, wait 15 seconds, and turn back on) to force the modem to re-register with the strongest cell tower.
2. **Switch or Reconnect to Wi-Fi (`Settings > Network`)**:
   - On **Get Smart** (Android): Swipe down from the top bar or open **Settings (Configurações) > Network & Internet (Rede e Internet) > Wi-Fi**, toggle **Wi-Fi ON**, select a stable 2.4 GHz business Wi-Fi network, and enter the password.
   - On **Get Clássica**: Press the **Menu / Function** key > **Configurações > Comunicação > Wi-Fi**, scan for available networks, and connect.
3. **Reset Dual-SIM 4G / Verify APN (`getnet.br`) for `OFFLINE_GPRS_TIMEOUT`**:
   - If the terminal telemetry reports `OFFLINE_GPRS_TIMEOUT` (stalled 4G GPRS data session), toggle **Airplane Mode (Modo Avião)** ON for 10 seconds and then OFF.
   - Open **Settings > Mobile Network > Access Point Names (APN)** and verify the APN is set to **`getnet.br`** (or carrier-specific Getnet M2M APN) and that **Mobile Data** is enabled on the primary SIM slot.
   - If using **Get Smart** with Dual-SIM, switch the active data SIM slot to the secondary carrier in **Settings > SIM Cards**.
4. **Physical SIM Reseating & Support Escalation**:
   - Power off the **POS Terminal**, open the rear compartment, gently clean and reseat the Getnet SIM card, and power back on.
   - If the terminal remains offline on both **Wi-Fi** and **4G (`getnet.br`)**, request a SIM/terminal swap via a Getnet **Support Ticket**.
